Connect cards with dependency arrows

Shared by Nick Palenchar on November 1, 2026
1. Demo2. Addendum of Oct 31 at 23:293. Addendum of Nov 1 at 06:26

Draw dependencies directly between cards using dots that appear as the pointer approaches. A ghost arrow follows the pointer and snaps to its target; the dependent card then waits for its prerequisite. Manual arrows can be removed after confirmation, while projects keep the ordering decided from their plans.

Addendum of Oct 31 at 23:29

Choose the target edge

Move the pointer around a target card to attach a dependency arrow to its nearest edge. The connection keeps the chosen attachment points when the page reloads or the cards move, and can still be removed from the arrow.

Addendum of Nov 1 at 06:26

Queue connected cards

Connect cards to specify which work comes first. Starting a dependent card puts it in the queue until its prerequisites are finished. You can still start it early after confirming a warning.
