Comparing agent ratings by model

Shared by Nick Palenchar on November 3, 2026
1. Demo2. Addendum of Nov 3 at 22:20

After approving an agent's work you can rate it with one to five stars. Obeya keeps each rating with the harness, model and effort that actually did the work. A new Agent performance dashboard puts those ratings side by side: one row per model and effort, with how many ratings it has, their average and how they split across the stars. You can also see every single rating as a dot, or the monthly average as a line. Rows are never ranked by score. Rows with few ratings are marked as such, and every rating links back to the card it is about.

Addendum of Nov 3 at 22:20

Agent ratings: narrower bars, dots sized by age

Two refinements to the agent performance dashboard. The star bars in the table keep to a moderate width. In the view showing each rating, the dots scatter in a loose cloud around their star value, and newer ratings get larger dots, from the last three days down to tiny dots for ratings older than two months.
