Merge into the card furthest along

Shared by Nick Palenchar on November 5, 2026

Start from a planned task or proposal and choose a partner at any stage. Review covered work and any proposed follow-up before approving the merge. Work already in progress, finished or archived stays intact, and extra requirements become planned follow-up tasks. Two unworked cards can still become one combined task.
