Cards get addresses of their own

Shared by Nick Palenchar on November 1, 2026
1. Demo2. Addendum of Nov 1 at 07:47

Every canvas and every open card now has its own address, like /c/shop/card/118518, with a short code instead of a long ID. Reloading the page keeps the card open, and a link to a card can be shared or bookmarked. The browser's Back and Forward buttons step through the cards you looked at. Where one card mentions another, such as the cards a queued task waits for, the name is now a link that opens that card.

Card links in blue

Where one card names another, the name is now blue as well as underlined, so it reads as a link at a glance. The short code in a card's address is hexadecimal: the first six characters of the card's ID.
